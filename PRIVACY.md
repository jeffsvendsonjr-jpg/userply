# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 26, 2026_

userp.ly is a Chrome extension that shows date context on Google search results before you click.

## Scope

This extension currently operates on Google search result pages. It does not provide Bing or DuckDuckGo functionality in the current launch scope.

## Information stored locally

The extension stores limited information in your browser to operate correctly, such as:

- extension settings
- cached date-verification results
- an anonymous local identifier used for request handling

This information is stored locally in your browser and is not sold.

## Information sent to remote services

To provide core functionality, the extension may send limited data to remote services only when needed:

### License verification

The extension may contact `https://public-website-builder.replit.app/*` to verify license status and determine whether Pro features should be enabled.

### Date verification

The extension may contact `https://nihquqccvnfuaqsxyymj.supabase.co/*` to verify or estimate date context for search results.

Requests may include the result URL, a claimed date when available, and an anonymous local identifier generated by the extension.

## How information is used

Information is used only to:

- display date context on Google search results
- determine whether Pro-only features should be available
- improve extension reliability and request handling

## Data sale and advertising

userp.ly does not sell your personal information. userp.ly does not use your browsing data for advertising.

## Feature fallback

If license validation is invalid, expired, or unavailable, the extension falls back to Free behavior.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the extension changes. The published privacy policy and the shipped extension behavior should match.
